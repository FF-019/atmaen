# Medical content review

The protocols in `backend/app/services/ai/protocols.py` were written by the project team from public
first aid and poison-centre material (WHO, poison control centres, Saudi Red Crescent first aid
content). They have **not** been clinically validated.

Before any pilot with real families, a licensed physician (ideally a clinical toxicologist or pediatric
emergency physician) should review and sign off on:

- every protocol's steps, do-nots and warning signs
- the severity of each category and each chemical's hazard level
- the symptom escalation rules in `guidance.py`
- the honey step for button batteries (age limit, dose, timing)
- the wording of the disclaimer
